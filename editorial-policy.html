Editorial Policy.

How we make a briefing

Horizon Brief summaries are attributed, concise accounts of public source material. Daily Brief items may concern unrelated announcements; multiple publishers do not necessarily corroborate the same event. We are not the original publisher of linked announcements.

Sources and AI assistance

We use approved public sources with recorded links, timestamps and reuse conditions. Source text is untrusted data. AI helps prepare drafts using the supplied sources; it does not approve articles, invent sources or publish autonomously. Each paragraph identifies its supporting sources.

Human review and accountability

A named human reviewer authorizes publication after checking sources, claims and reuse rights. Articles display AI assistance, reviewer and publication/update dates. Material edits invalidate approval and require a new review.

Corrections and revision history

Send the article URL, disputed statement and supporting evidence to Contact. Confirmed corrections retain the original publication date, show the update and correction note, and preserve the previous version locally. No correction record does not mean that every claim is free from error.

Independence and samples

Advertisers do not control editorial decisions. Relevant conflicts or sponsorship must be disclosed before publication. We do not invent popularity, traffic or endorsements. Sample content demonstrates the interface, is labeled and excluded from production, RSS and search engine indexing.
