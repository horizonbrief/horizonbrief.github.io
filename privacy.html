HORIZON BRIEF / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy.

Browser features

The site code sets no cookies and provides no user accounts, comments, analytics or newsletter database. Article search runs in your browser; search terms are not sent to a search service. A search query in the page URL may appear in hosting request logs. localStorage stores only the theme preference, under horizon-theme; clear site data to remove it. No external fonts or tracking pixels are loaded.

Hosting and external links

Cloudflare delivers this public website and may process IP addresses and request metadata under its privacy policy. Hosting-provider log retention follows the provider’s policy and is separate from email retention. Source links visit other sites with their own policies.

Contact and retention

The operator uses the public name Horizon Brief and is based in China. The site brand is Horizon Brief; a public name does not assert incorporation or a registered office. Messages sent to Contact are received by the operator and processed by the email provider (QQ Mail for the configured qq.com address). They are used to respond, investigate corrections and handle privacy requests. Routine correspondence is deleted within 90 days after the matter closes, subject to unresolved disputes or legal obligations. Public correction records omit private correspondence and sender details. Do not send sensitive personal information. Access, correction and deletion rights depend on applicable law.

News drafting

DeepSeek receives selected public news titles and excerpts, including any people named in those sources, to prepare drafts. The site does not send visitor search terms, theme preferences or contact emails to DeepSeek. Source records, content revisions and token accounting are retained locally for editorial traceability, separately from visitor data. No DeepSeek code or key is delivered to readers’ browsers.

Advertising

No advertising scripts, advertising cookies or paid impressions are active. Reserved spaces make no requests to advertising providers. Before enabling a provider, this page must disclose its processing, retention and any required consent or opt-out controls.
