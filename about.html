About.

A wider view of the world

Horizon Brief is an independent English briefing for curious readers. We cover world affairs, technology, business, science and climate with concise context and links to the information behind each story.

Sources first, people accountable

Our draft pipeline discovers recent topics through public news and trend feeds, then writes original briefings from limited factual notes and short public excerpts. It records publisher timestamps, rejects missing or suspicious sources, and attributes every paragraph. AI assists drafting. Publication follows a named editor’s review or the owner’s standing authorization for automated source, fact, reuse-rights and risk checks; each article identifies its review method. Two publishers in a briefing do not necessarily corroborate the same event.

Corrections and revisions

If a report needs correction, we preserve its original publication time, display the correction note and update time, and retain a revision record. Contact the editorial desk with the article link, disputed statement and supporting source. We investigate and update confirmed errors.

Samples and advertising

Editorial samples demonstrate the reading experience and are clearly labeled. They are excluded from production builds, RSS and search engine indexes. Advertising placements are disabled until official scripts and appropriate privacy disclosures are configured.

Ownership and contact

The operator uses the public name Horizon Brief and is based in China. The site brand is Horizon Brief; a public name does not assert incorporation or a registered office. Editorial questions, corrections and privacy requests can be sent through Contact.
