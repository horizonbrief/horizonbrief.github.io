Rays Take AL Pennant, Set Up First-Title World Series Against Brewers

Tampa Bay closed out Cleveland in Game 6 of the ALCS and will meet Milwaukee in a Fall Classic where neither club has won a championship. Game 1 is Friday in Milwaukee.

Horizon Brief summary

The Tampa Bay Rays secured the American League pennant by defeating the Cleveland Guardians 7-3 in Game 6 of the ALCS on Monday night, NPR reported. It is the franchise's third pennant across 29 major league seasons.[S20]

Díaz and Junior Caminero both connected for two-run home runs in the clincher. Freddy Peralta worked 5 1/3 innings for Tampa Bay. Caminero had 42 home runs during the regular season but required 33 at-bats before hitting his first of October, according to NPR.[S20]

The Rays now meet the National League champion Milwaukee Brewers. According to NPR, it is the first World Series in which both participants are chasing their first title since 1980, when the Philadelphia Phillies defeated the Kansas City Royals. Game 1 is set for Friday night in Milwaukee.[S20]

Tampa Bay's path to the ALCS included a Division Series sweep of the New York Yankees. Once in the championship series, the Rays trailed the Guardians 1-0 and then 2-1 before winning three consecutive games to take the pennant.[S20]
