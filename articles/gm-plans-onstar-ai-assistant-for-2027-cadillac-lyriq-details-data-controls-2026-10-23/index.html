GM Plans OnStar AI Assistant for 2027 Cadillac Lyriq, Details Data Controls

GM says the assistant will debut in the coming months. Its data-sharing statements include consent controls and possible limits on features when customers decline to share information.

Horizon Brief summary

GM plans to introduce its OnStar AI Assistant with the 2027 Cadillac Lyriq EV, before extending it to additional vehicles, TechCrunch reports. The company placed the debut in the coming months when it outlined the plan at Wednesday's GM Forward event in New York.[S501]

The proposal brings a personal AI assistant into OnStar, a subscription service launched 30 years ago. GM presented it alongside updates concerning vehicle software, EVs, automated driving and electrical architecture.[S501]

Data handling forms another part of GM's account of the refresh. The company says customers will be able to choose what they share. According to TechCrunch, GM says recordings are encrypted and subject to time limits; it also says neither GM nor OnStar can access Connected Camera recordings.[S501]

A company spokesperson also explained the conditions for sharing with third parties: information can be supplied when a particular function needs it and the customer has consented. Permissions can be changed or withdrawn. According to GM, withholding some data may restrict access to the associated features, while leaving other OnStar services operating.[S501]

TechCrunch also links the announcement to an FTC order finalized earlier this year. Under that order, GM and OnStar are barred from sending certain consumer information to consumer reporting agencies. The automaker must give consumers greater transparency and secure explicit consent for certain collection practices, the report says.[S501]

The AI plans follow several earlier changes. GM widened access to its OnStar Guardian app in 2021, making crash detection, location sharing and emergency help available to people with compatible phones, including those who did not own its vehicles. In the following year it adopted Google Cloud's Dialogflow for spoken requests. The 2023 expansion added responses to routine questions, including route and navigation help.[S501]

TechCrunch describes that earlier work as part of GM's plan for a $25 billion subscription business by 2030. Separately, GM said last year that Google's Gemini would come to its vehicles in 2026.[S501]
