Canva Launches Canva Craft, Its First In-House Image Generation Model

The design platform says the model, built by its research team with contributions from Leonardo.ai researchers, is available now through the Dream Lab tab, with API access planned.

Horizon Brief summary

Canva has introduced Canva Craft, its first image generation model, TechCrunch reported. The design platform built the model within its own research team and intends it to compete with comparable image generators from Google, OpenAI, ByteDance and Meta, according to the report.[S501]

TechCrunch describes the generation process as two distinct phases. In the first, Canva Craft reads the user's prompt and drafts a composition plan covering how elements are positioned relative to one another, their comparative sizes, the visual hierarchy of the image and the information needed to render any text. The second phase turns that plan into the finished image, which can reach up to 2K resolution and be produced in as many as 30 aspect ratios for use across different platforms.[S501]

According to the report, users would also be able to make targeted edits to an image without altering the rest of it. Images produced with Canva Craft could then be turned into layered designs through Canva Magic, the company's tool for converting an image into an editable design.[S501]

Canva Craft is available on Canva's platform to all users, who can open it from the Dream Lab tab, TechCrunch reported. The company also plans to offer the model through the Canva API, a future step rather than a current feature.[S501]

The release builds on Canva's 2024 acquisition of Leonardo.ai, a media generation platform. Canva said researchers from Leonardo took part in developing the new model, according to TechCrunch. The report also noted that the company was on track for $4 billion in revenue last year.[S501]
