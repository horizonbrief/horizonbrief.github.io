Meta Plans New Non-Voting Share Class to Help Fund AI Push

The company confirmed on its third-quarter earnings call that it intends to distribute a new class of non-voting stock to existing shareholders, with trading expected to begin in 2027.

Horizon Brief summary

Meta plans to create a new class of non-voting stock that could help finance its artificial intelligence buildout, a plan the company confirmed during its third-quarter earnings call on Wednesday. The move was first reported by Semafor, according to TechCrunch, which said the shares would carry no vote on company decisions.[S24]

Finance chief Susan Li said existing shareholders would receive the planned non-voting capital stock through a stock dividend, TechCrunch reported. The Verge identified potential acquisitions and employee compensation as additional uses for the shares.[S24][S36]

The share plan arrives alongside quarterly results that TechCrunch described as strong and ahead of Wall Street expectations. Costs and expenses rose as well, by 68% year over year, according to the same report.[S24]

Meta's Reality Labs division, which houses its AR glasses, VR headsets and VR software, continues to lose money, though TechCrunch reported the losses are smaller than they once were. The company said in its earnings report that it expects the division's losses to shrink from 2025 levels to between $18 billion and $19 billion.[S24]

Meta has expanded its AI lineup over the past year. TechCrunch reported that the company released several models, including Muse Spark and Muse Glimmer earlier this year, and in recent weeks added Muse Wonder and Muse Spark Open, an open-weight model whose underlying parameters are publicly available for outsiders to use and build on.[S24]

Zuckerberg also reported uptake of Meta's personal agents during the call. According to TechCrunch, he said more than 10 million people had set them up in the U.S. and Canada over a month and a half.[S24]

The Verge reported that trading in the new share class would begin in 2027. The share distribution remains a company plan, as described by Li in the account from TechCrunch.[S24][S36]
