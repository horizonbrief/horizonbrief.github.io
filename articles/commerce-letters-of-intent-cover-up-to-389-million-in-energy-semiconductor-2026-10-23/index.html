Commerce Letters of Intent Cover Up to $389 Million in Energy Semiconductor R&D

Four companies are named for potential CHIPS Act awards covering fusion power electronics, gallium nitride devices and ultra-high-purity quartz, with Helion's share the largest at up to $175 million.

Horizon Brief summary

The Department of Commerce has signed letters of intent with four companies that would provide up to $389 million in federal research and development awards under the CHIPS and Science Act, the National Institute of Standards and Technology announced. The proposed funding is aimed at semiconductors and materials involved in controlling, converting and delivering power.[S201][S202]

The four recipients named are Helion, Commonwealth Fusion Systems, Vertical Semiconductor and Sibelco. Each figure is a maximum described in a letter of intent, not a completed award.[S201][S202]

Helion's share would be the largest, at up to $175 million, for developing and scaling domestic work on high-voltage power semiconductors and advanced energy power modules built for pulsed-power use with high peak currents in fusion generation. NIST said the devices would manage power for the magnets that drive Helion's fusion generators, including controlling and harvesting energy from them.[S201][S202]

Commonwealth Fusion Systems would receive up to $85 million for R&D on several electronics categories: semiconductors for radio-frequency plasma heating, power electronics, embedded electronics used in fusion diagnostics, and components designed to withstand radiation.[S201][S202]

Vertical Semiconductor is named for up to $73 million to advance its vertical gallium nitride power semiconductors, which the agency said could make power delivery for advanced computing more efficient and compact. The work would also include demonstrating the technology for what the announcement called the world's first 2.5 MW super intelligence data center rack.[S201][S202]

Sibelco would receive up to $56 million for R&D on purification and finishing methods for ultra-high-purity quartz. The material is used in crucibles for crystal growth, in processing glassware and in other hardware across the semiconductor value chain, and NIST said the work is meant to supply next-generation ultra-high-purity quartz to support the domestic supply chain.[S201][S202]

According to the agency, better power converter performance can bring higher power efficiency, improved grid stability and lower thermal losses.[S201][S202]

TechCrunch described the proposed Helion support as a three-year CHIPS Act award. It described the Biden-era law as intended to strengthen U.S. research, development and manufacturing of semiconductor modules. Helion announced the funding on Thursday, the publication reported.[S201][S202]

Helion's existing power devices lose roughly 5% to 10% of the electricity passing through them, according to TechCrunch. The report said the funding would help the company build automated capacity for semiconductor modules, packaging and testing to supply the fusion machine now under construction.[S201][S202]

TechCrunch quoted Helion's Kirtley saying no U.S. manufacturers currently perform that work. The report also said more than $100 million would be set aside for semiconductor and advanced packaging R&D in Washington State, while about $80 million would support R&D in Vermont on advanced semiconductor tooling, design and process development with GlobalFoundries.[S201][S202]

Separately, TechCrunch reported that GlobalFoundries has received more than $80 million from the federal government to advance manufacturing of gallium-nitride semiconductors, a type widely used in power electronics.[S201][S202]

Helion is working to finish its first power plant, Orion, in time for a 2028 deadline to supply Microsoft with electricity from the grid, TechCrunch reported. Kirtley acknowledged that the R&D funded by the award would not reach Orion in time for its launch, because federal contracts can take months to negotiate, according to the publication. He said the work could improve efficiency at later Helion plants and their ability to compete in electricity markets.[S201][S202]

TechCrunch also reported that Helion announced a new $50 million private investment alongside the federal funding but did not identify the investor. The company has raised more than $3.2 billion in total, making it the second-best-funded fusion startup, the publication reported.[S201][S202]

Helion says its approach, known as direct energy conversion, will help future reactors generate more power than they consume, a threshold any fusion plant must meet to be commercially viable, according to TechCrunch.[S201][S202]

Bill Frauenhofer, who leads semiconductor investment and innovation at Commerce, said in a press release that the award would help convert and deliver power to critical applications in defense, grid transmission and advanced energy generation, TechCrunch reported. Despite possible defense contracts, Kirtley said Helion remains focused on putting power on the grid, telling the publication that domestic energy supply, national security through energy dominance and energy security are its main focus.[S201][S202]
